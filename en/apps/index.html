Lensori

Find your photos again with natural language and reference images.

Features App Store

What It Solves

Lensori helps you find the moments you remember in a large photo library: no exact date, album, or filename required. You can also use one photo as a reference to find similar photos.

Core Features

  • Search photos by natural language, scene, time, or place.
  • Find similar photos with a reference image.
  • Browse results in a focused photo viewer.
  • Create 3D Memory clips after upgrading to Pro.

Technical Highlights

  • On-device CLIP visual retrieval: indexes and searches your photo library locally, without accounts or server uploads.
  • On-device translation model: translates Chinese and Japanese queries into English, then matches them against local photo embeddings.
  • On-device depth model: generates 3D Memory videos from photos.
